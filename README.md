# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day

One image that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in world news: 40 stories
across six continents — a first-ever trilateral in Shenzhen, a genocide verdict
in The Hague, a record IPO on Wall Street, and the rest of the day.

Layout: the world map is the hero, with numbered pins for the twelve biggest
stories, three text-led leads, nine more stories from around the world, and
28 one-line briefs. Every story was reported on 19 November 2026; disputed
claims are attributed. Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera.
Original drawings.

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 19 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the committed `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. `render.py` (Pillow, build time only)
draws `entry.png` from the story list in that file.
